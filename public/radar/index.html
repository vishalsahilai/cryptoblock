TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

How a setup qualifies

The experimental 2-day, 3-day and 7-day AI/ML outlooks uses k-nearest historical analogues. Daily features include 7/14/28-day log momentum, 14-day return volatility, gain/loss balance, daily range, and relative volume. Distances are standardized using only past training features. Candidates are spaced by the selected forecast horizon and their complete forward outcomes must be known at the forecast origin. The weighted median is the point estimate; weighted 10th/90th percentiles show historical analogue outcomes, not a guaranteed confidence interval. Up to 26 earlier nonoverlapping horizon-specific tests report direction hit rate, mean absolute endpoint error, and range coverage, with unchanged-price and always-up baselines. At least 210 closed consecutive daily candles are required. No future accuracy is promised. Demo forecasts use synthetic history.

We analyze closed candles. Trend is the 9/21 EMA relationship; a gap smaller than 0.15 ATR is neutral. An order-block candidate is the last opposite candle before a displacement candle closes beyond the prior 10-candle range. Later closes beyond its far edge invalidate it.

Score combines trend separation (30), aligned five-candle momentum (20), relative volume (15), an active matching zone (20), and a directional candle closing back outside the zone after a retest (15). Open interest, funding and taker delta are context, not proof of institutional intent.

Ready requires score ≥65, a current closed-candle retest and confirmation, and ≥1.5 reward/risk to the nearest qualifying swing target. Entry is that candle’s close; stop is beyond the zone by 0.2 ATR. Otherwise the scanner says Wait. Stale feeds disable Ready.

Radar and detail charts show the selected viewing range. The selector offers 15 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, 1 week, 1 calendar month and 6 calendar months, with automatic candle granularity. The 1-second live mode builds real OHLC bars from observed public trades, displays up to two minutes of observed bars and does not fabricate missing seconds. Setup calculations in that mode use one-minute closed candles. Historical order-block analysis warms up on earlier candles outside the visible window. The weekly forecast always uses daily history. The range changes price history only; buying/selling pressure still uses its separately labelled recent trade sample. Price direction is separate from taker flow. The live universe is the 60 USDT perpetual symbols with highest exchange-reported 24h turnover at scan start. Buy-pressure rankings contain coins above 50% taker buy volume; sell-pressure rankings contain coins above 50% sell volume. Up to 20 are shown per side, ordered by share, with sampled USDT volume as tie-breaker. Stale samples do not qualify. Sparse results are not filled with invented entries. Market-wide flow sums sampled USDT trade notional, not unique people or total capital inflow.

Buying/selling bars show each side’s share of base-coin taker volume in the latest up to 1,000 observed trades within five minutes. REST seeds recent trades and WebSocket trades update them; execution IDs prevent duplicates. The exact sample count and observed duration are shown. It is a sample, not complete five-minute history, not unique people, and not long/short account positioning. No trades means no percentage. Samples older than 30 seconds are labelled stale.

VWAP resets at 00:00 UTC. Trade delta covers only trades received during this browser session. Book imbalance uses the displayed top 50 bid/ask quantities. Zones and signals are calculated by our own rules, not Order-Block.com’s private engine. No win rate has been established.
